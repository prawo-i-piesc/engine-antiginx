    Constants¶

    UnknownDayCount is what a day counter holds when the date it derives from was not published, which registries increasingly do for privacy reasons.

    const UnknownDayCount = -1

    maxRDAPResponseSize caps how much of an RDAP response is read.

    const maxRDAPResponseSize = 1 << 20

    func (*Collector) lookupZone¶

    lookupZone queries everything the DNS can say about the hostname and its zone in one batch, and splits the answers into the resolution and mail policy sections.

    func (*Collector) probeWildcard¶

    func (c *Collector) probeWildcard(ctx context.Context, domain string) bool

    probeWildcard reports whether the zone answers for a name nobody ever configured. It shares the deadline of the batch it is issued with, because it depends on none of the other answers and so has no reason to cost the scan a second timeout of its own.

    func (c vCard) organization() string
